A Trusted Wholesale Seafood Partner in Rochester

JD & Sons is a wholesale seafood distributor serving restaurants, retail markets, and food service operators throughout the region. We supply a dedicated line of white fish, salmon, and breaded seafood products, each prepared to our customers' individual specifications.

Our team is committed to consistent quality, dependable service, and lasting business relationships. Every order is prepared at our facility at 100 International Blvd and held to the same standards of care, food safety, and attention to detail.

Local Expertise, Regional Reach

Based in Rochester, we understand the needs of the regional food service market and the importance of a reliable supply partner.

Quality and Food Safety

As a HACCP-certified facility, we inspect, handle, and store every product to strict quality and food safety standards, from receiving through final delivery.
